Colorado's Front Range has a quietly excellent independent bookstore scene, and
it deserves a map. This site is my attempt to keep track of the shops worth your
time — what each one is good at, where to find it, and what's happening there.

Every store gets an honest write-up, an address with directions, and a pin on
[the map](/bookstores). Some posts here will be about a specific shop; others
(like this one) are just notes on the scene. Either way, the goal is simple: get
you to **please read something** — ideally bought from a shop within a short
drive.

If you run or love a store I've missed, [tell me about it](/contact).
