Once you start reading books as a mission, you quickly need more books and more people to talk to about books. So I started reviewing bookstores. One of the first I reviewed was Old Fire House Books in Fort Collins, CO. While writing these blogs I went back and watched my early videos. Ouch. Another goal started to take shape: not only should people be reading more, but I want them to be conscientious of where they get their books. I wondered whether I should just post about bookstores all over the world, since I had also covered Librerías Kaiku in Ensenada, Mexico. But a focus came into view when I learned that the Front Range runs from Cheyenne, Wyoming, down to Santa Fe, New Mexico. So that's what I plan to cover (stand by for the occasional one-off).

In the future I also plan to cover libraries, because buying all the books I want in life is unsustainable. Libraries are also filled with people who know about books and want to talk about books. They put on amazing book clubs and book-related events, and they need our support too. That's also why I wanted to build a one-stop shop for finding book events in our area.

Read well.
