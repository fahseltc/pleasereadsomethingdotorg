## Greetings and welcome.

My name is Lucy, and in 2025 I found myself without a job for the first time since I was 16, and in a new city because of my husband's job. Not sure what was next and needing something to do, I started reviewing books on YouTube. The first book I read was Ben Riggs' [Slay the Dragon](https://bookshop.org/p/books/slaying-the-dragon-a-secret-history-of-dungeons-dragons-ben-riggs/d1d9bd1c88b1dc93?ean=9781250819475&affiliate=130147). I loved it, and it got me interested in convincing people to read more. So I kept at it. My first goal was to avoid moving more books than I had to while we looked for a place to live in the new city, which meant reading and giving away as much as I could. It worked. I read and gave away just over 20 inches of books.

I have now been reading and reviewing books for a year. I have loved many of them, and some I have not. I added bookstore reviews as I went. I have almost 100 followers on YouTube, I have started a storefront on Bookshop.org, and my favorite part has been building out this website to help link books to people and book people to each other. So check it out and find your book life here in the Front Range.

### So: read it, don't read it, but please read something.

Read Well,
# Lucy
