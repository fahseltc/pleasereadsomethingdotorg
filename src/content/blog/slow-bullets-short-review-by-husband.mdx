[*Slow Bullets*](https://bookshop.org/p/books/slow-bullets-alastair-reynolds/8caeb0fdd11dec31?ean=9781616961930) is a short but dramatic sci-fi refresher coming in just under 200 pages. I read it in about a week and, as usual, am very interested in any story involving a spaceship; particularly stories about abandoned or derelict ones.

<BlogImage src="/images/blog/slow-bullets.jpg" alt="Close-up of the book" caption="The copy I bought used for $8" size="medium" />

The story is told from the perspective of Scur, a soldier on one side of a large space conflict who awakens from long-term sleep to find herself on a prison ship full of innocent soldiers, war criminals and civilians lost in space with no real leader.

It tells a story of revenge, making do with an uncertain future and the lengths we might go to keep on going when hope might be lost. I really enjoyed the concept of a 'slow bullet' which is a piece of technology that gets injected into people and inchworms its way inside of you. It's used as a sort of identification / history record of a person contained within themselves and is both gross and fascinating.

I would highly recommend squeezing this short read between your larger books. It  tells a nice self-contained story and doesn't overstay its welcome.

Read it, don't read it, just please read something!

-- Husband
